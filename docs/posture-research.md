# Why the programme looks the way it does

Research notes behind the home programme (checked 2026-11-02). The app's Rules tab carries the short version.

## What sitting does, and what the trials say fixes it

| Pattern | Tight / overactive | Weak / inhibited | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forward head, rounded shoulders, upper-back hump ("upper crossed") | pecs, upper traps, levator scapulae, suboccipitals | deep neck flexors, lower and middle traps, serratus anterior | 8-week supervised programme, 3×/week: side-lying external rotation, diagonal flexion, prone V/T/W, abduction — forward-head angle 46.7→39.5°, shoulder angle 54.4→45.5°, kyphosis 47.9→36.3°, held at 1-month follow-up ([RCT, PMC7692548](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7692548/)). Meta-analysis: posture angles improve reliably; pain and function less consistently ([PubMed 42210327](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42210327/)). |
| Anterior pelvic tilt, lumbar arch ("lower crossed") | hip flexors, lumbar erectors | glutes, abdominals | 8-week NASM-style programme (inhibit → stretch → activate → integrate): lumbar lordosis 50.0→44.0°, higher glute-max activity vs control ([RCT, PMC12959714](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12959714/)). Systematic review: stretching + strengthening (Janda) effective ([review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/362224732_Treatment_strategies_for_lower_cross_syndrome_A_systematic_review)). |
| Neck/shoulder pain in office workers | — | shoulder abductors / scapular muscles | 2 minutes a day of one band exercise to near-failure, 5×/week for 10 weeks: pain down ~40% vs control ([RCT, PMC3892746](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3892746/)); 2 min and 12 min a day worked about equally well ([Andersen 2011](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/e54bd1eb41e5578453adbef251375ab32994922d)). |
| Deep neck flexor endurance | — | longus colli / capitis | Home protocol 3 × 10 chin tucks × 10 s hold daily improved craniovertebral angle ([DUT dissertation](https://openscholar.dut.ac.za/handle/10321/1210)). |
| Lower trap / serratus activation | — | — | Prone Y strongest for lower trap; wall slide and side-lying external rotation close behind and easier early on; push-up plus for serratus ([Otago EMG](https://ourarchive.otago.ac.nz/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Variation-of-muscle-recruitment-during-exercises/9926495568701891), [EMG summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4085196)). |

## How that maps onto the week

- **Mon (upper back & neck):** band-bar seated row, mini-band pulldown, mini-band W (external rotation + W), prone Y-T-W, supine chin tuck + lift (10 s holds), reverse crunch, doorway pec stretch.
- **Tue (hips & glutes):** warm-up stretches first (90/90, hip flexor), then mini-band bridge, supported split squat, band-bar RDL, chair squat / wall sit, lateral walk or clamshell, ab wheel, half-kneeling hip flexor stretch.
- **Wed (core & mobility):** McGill curl-up, side plank, bird dog, shoulder taps, dead bug, cat-cow / open book / child's pose.
- **Thu (push–pull & arms):** push-up, band-bar bent-over row, wall slide with lift-off (replaces the press), mini-band pull-apart, band-bar curl, push-up plus, crunch, child's pose.
- **Fri (posterior chain & carries):** band-bar deadlift, stair step-up, prone swimmers, loaded carry, pull-aparts, cross-body crunch, hamstring + couch stretch.
- **Every workday (Desk breaks):** chin tucks 10 × 10 s and one ~2-minute set of mini-band pull-aparts, plus the pec, hip flexor and thoracic stretches.

## Dosage

- 8 weeks, 3 sets of 10–15 for strength moves once past the learning weeks; stretches 30–45 s; holds 10 s rising to 15 s (the trials progressed 10 s × 7 → 15 s × 10 in two weeks).
- Small daily amounts beat occasional long sessions for the neck and shoulders.
- Band bar: its cords only reach hip to chest height, so no pressing or squatting with it (RDL, deadlift, rows, curls only).

## Limits of the evidence

Most trials are small (15–35 per group), young adults, 6–10 weeks, and measure posture angles rather than long-term pain. None tests this exact combination. The programme is a reasonable application of them, not a prescription.
