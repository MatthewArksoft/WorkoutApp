Desk Reset Program

Demo model

Reminders · weekdays 17:30

Five home sessions of about 45 minutes, built on what the research on desk posture supports: strengthen the deep neck flexors, lower traps, serratus, glutes and abs, stretch the chest and hip flexors, and add a two-minute dose at the desk every workday. All you need is a yoga mat, mini bands, an ab wheel and a band bar. Each session ends with a walk or a bike ride.

Mon–Fri · weekends open ~45 min incl. cardio Home · no gym Pull : push ≈ 2 : 1
